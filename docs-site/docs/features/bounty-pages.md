# bounty pages 📄

Every bounty on poidh has its own dedicated page.

This is where contributors collaborate, submit work, review claims, track activity, and follow the progress of a bounty from creation to completion.

A bounty page brings together everything related to a single bounty in one place.

---

## bounty details

At the top of every bounty page you'll find the key information about the bounty.

This includes:

* The bounty title and description
* The reward amount
* The blockchain the bounty is deployed on
* The bounty creator

Together, these details help contributors quickly understand what work is being requested and where it lives onchain.

## bounty creator

Every bounty displays the wallet that created it.

Clicking the creator's profile lets you learn more about them before deciding whether to participate.

Depending on what they've chosen to share, a profile may include:

* Linked social accounts
* Previous bounties they've created
* Claims they've submitted
* Their activity across poidh

Checking a creator's profile can provide valuable context, especially when participating in larger or long-running community initiatives.

## reward amount and chain

Every bounty clearly displays:

* The total reward available
* The token being used
* The blockchain where the bounty exists

This makes it easy to know exactly what you're working toward before submitting a claim.

If you're unfamiliar with the selected network, you'll also know which wallet network you'll need when interacting with the bounty.

---

## contributors

The **Contributors** tab shows everyone who has participated in a bounty.

This includes people who have:

* Funded the bounty
* Boosted the reward
* Submitted claims
* Contributed in other ways

It's a useful way to see who is helping drive a bounty forward and understand how many people are involved in a particular effort.

[SCREENSHOT: Contributors tab]

---

## transaction history

Every bounty includes a **Transactions** tab that records important activity throughout its lifetime.

Rather than hiding what happened behind the scenes, poidh provides a transparent history of the bounty's progression.

Depending on the bounty, you'll see events such as:

* Bounty creation
* Reward boosts
* New claim submissions
* Claims entering voting
* Voting results
* Completed payouts
* Other important protocol events

Each blockchain transaction links directly to the appropriate block explorer, making it easy to independently verify every onchain action.

## sharing a bounty

Want to bring more people into a bounty?

Use the **Share Bounty** button.

This generates a shareable link you can post anywhere, making it easy to invite contributors, attract additional funding, or spread the word about work that needs to be completed.

The more visibility a bounty receives, the more likely it is to attract high-quality contributions.

[SCREENSHOT: Share button]

---

## claims

The **Claims** section is where contributors submit evidence that they've completed the requested work.

Claims are displayed in reverse chronological order, with the newest submissions appearing first.

Each claim contains the contributor's evidence and any discussion related to that submission.

From here, community members can review claims, participate in voting when applicable, and follow the progress of each submission.

[SCREENSHOT: Claims list]

---

## comments

Every bounty has a comment section for discussion.

Comments allow contributors to ask questions, provide updates, clarify requirements, or discuss submitted work.

To leave a comment, simply connect your wallet and sign a message.

Signing proves ownership of your wallet without creating an onchain transaction or requiring any gas fees.

[SCREENSHOT: Comments section]

---

## everything in one place

A bounty page is designed to provide complete transparency throughout the lifecycle of a bounty.

Whether you're evaluating the creator, reviewing submitted work, tracking onchain activity, or participating in discussions, everything you need is available from a single page.

As poidh continues to evolve, bounty pages will gain additional tools that make coordinating, reviewing, and completing work even easier.
