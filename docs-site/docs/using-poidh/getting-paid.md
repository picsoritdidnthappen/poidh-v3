## where to claim your funds

Rewards are not sent automatically.

Instead, poidh uses a **pull payment** model, which means you claim your rewards yourself.

To collect your payout:

1. Connect the wallet that submitted the winning claim.
2. Navigate to your profile page.
3. Claim your available balance.

<img width="600" alt="claiming funds" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2b1e9663-754e-473d-97b3-487472bd5ab1" />

This approach is more secure than automatically sending funds during bounty completion and gives you full control over when you receive your payout.

---

## claiming your reward

When you claim your rewards, your wallet will ask you to approve a transaction.

Once the transaction is confirmed, your reward will be transferred directly to your wallet.

That's it.

Your bounty has now been fully completed.

---

## protocol fee

poidh charges a flat **2.5% protocol fee** on completed bounties.

For example:

| Total Bounty | Protocol Fee | Claimant Receives |
| ------------ | -----------: | ----------------: |
| 1.00 ETH     |    0.025 ETH |         0.975 ETH |
| 100 DEGEN    |    2.5 DEGEN |        97.5 DEGEN |

The protocol fee is deducted automatically when the bounty is completed.

You don't need to calculate anything yourself.

---

## you'll also pay a network gas fee

Claiming your reward is an onchain transaction.

That means you'll also pay the normal network gas fee for whichever chain the bounty was created on.

For example:

* Ethereum Mainnet rewards require ETH for gas.
* Base rewards require ETH on Base for gas.
* Arbitrum rewards require ETH on Arbitrum for gas.
* DEGEN Chain rewards require ETH on DEGEN Chain for gas.

The gas fee is separate from the 2.5% protocol fee.

It goes to the network processing your transaction—not to poidh.

---

## why rewards are claimed instead of automatically sent

You might wonder why poidh asks winners to claim their rewards instead of automatically sending them.

The answer is simple:

**Pull payments are safer.**

Rather than having the protocol send funds during the completion process, rewards become available for withdrawal by the winning claimant.

This design improves the security of the protocol while ensuring only the correct wallet can claim the funds.

---

## your wallet matters

Rewards can only be claimed by the wallet that submitted the winning claim.

Make sure you're connected with the correct wallet before attempting to claim your funds.
